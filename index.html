About Us

Your Local Power Experts

At Performance Energy, we provide dependable backup power solutions for both homes and businesses throughout Baton Rouge and the surrounding areas. As a full-service Generac, Briggs and Stratton, Kohler and Champion Power Equipment dealer, our certified technicians are trained to handle everything from installation to maintenance with precision and care. Whether you’re protecting your family during a storm or keeping your business running without interruption, we’re here to make sure you’re always powered up.

We specialize in automatic standby generators that offer seamless transitions when outages occur—giving you peace of mind when you need it most. Our team stands behind every project with post-installation support, warranty service, and rapid emergency response. From homes to commercial facilities, Performance Energy Solutions is your trusted partner in modern energy security. Powering your future, one connection at a time.
